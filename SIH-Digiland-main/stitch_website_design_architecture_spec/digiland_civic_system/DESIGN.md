## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an authoritative, institutional, and calm aesthetic tailored for a critical civic mission: AI-assisted land record digitization, legal auditability, and cadastral spatial validation. It communicates absolute legal certainty, transparency, and bureaucratic reliability, balancing the gravity of historical public record-keeping with modern cloud productivity tools.

The interface serves revenue administration personnel—ranging from ground-level Patwaris and clerical document transcribers to verifying Tehsildars and district magistrates. Its primary emotional impact is trustworthy composure: cognitive fatigue must be minimized over long shifts of detailed human-in-the-loop validation. The visual style marries **Corporate / Modern** governance architecture with **Data-Dense Utility**. Visual clarity relies on structural alignment, subdued paper-inspired backdrops, crisp borders, and status-driven spatial grouping rather than decorative shadows or excessive layering.

## Colors

The color system combines deep forest greens, mineral ochre, and warm parchment neutrals. Color serves functional and diagnostic purposes:

- **Primary Green (`#1B5E3A` / dark `#5FBE8A`)**: Represents lawful validation, territorial sovereignty, and state sanction. Used for primary calls-to-action, validated polygon outlines, and key brand anchors.
- **Secondary Ochre (`#C17817` / dark `#E0A94D`)**: Grounded in natural clay, earth, and physical cadastral surveying. Directs focus to document inspection bounding boxes, drag-and-drop targets, and attention anchors.
- **Tertiary Blue (`#1B6FA8` / dark `#6FB1E0`)**: Denotes institutional trust, system linkages, hyperlinked entity references, and cryptographic audit proofs.
- **Neutral Palette (`#F7F5F0` / `#E0DED5` / `#1F2A24`)**: Replaces sterile stark white with warm, paper-inspired foundational tones to lessen eye strain during multi-hour verification sessions.

### Semantic Validation Mapping
Colors strictly reflect algorithmic confidence tiers:
- **Success / Valid (`#2E7D32`)**: Confidence ≥ 85%, rule clearance, cadastral match.
- **Warning / Flagged (`#ED8936`)**: Confidence 60%–84%, OCR discrepancy, requires human intervention.
- **Error / Critical (`#C0392B`)**: Confidence < 60%, geometric parcel overlap, signature or seal mismatch.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses three typefaces for distinct roles:
1. **Poppins (Medium 500 / SemiBold 600)**: Serves for structural anchors, top navigation, card titles, section headings, and primary buttons. Its geometric forms provide an accessible, official look.
2. **Inter (Regular 400 / Medium 500)**: Used for data-dense tabular rows, extracted form fields, tooltips, and general body reading. Inter preserves high legibility across small sizes within dense data grids.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Medium 500)**: Handles all cryptographic identifiers, Unique Land Parcel Identification Numbers (ULPIN), Khasra/Khata indices, coordinates, and SHA-256 digital seals. It eliminates character confusion between `0` and `O`, or `1`, `l`, and `I`.

When rendering non-Latin vernacular documentation (e.g., Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu), text seamlessly pairs with the corresponding **Noto Sans** script family, maintaining consistent optical weight and baseline balance against Western characters.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8px base grid** (supplemented with a 4px sub-grid for compact data density) across all layouts.

### Structural Framework
- **Shell Structure**: Fixed 256px (`16rem`) left sidebar paired with a 64px (`4rem`) utility topbar housing administrative profile details, system telemetry, and contextual search.
- **Verification Workspace**: A 50/50 dual-pane viewport configuration. The left pane provides an interactive canvas displaying scanned documents with coordinate overlays; the right pane renders an aligned form of extracted record attributes.
- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Desktop (>= 1280px)`: Full dual-pane split-screen verification with persistent sidebar and dense 4-column metric rows.
  - `Tablet (768px - 1279px)`: Collapsible icon-only sidebar (64px), single-column stacked verification mode with toggleable "Source vs Data" tabs.
  - `Mobile (< 768px)`: Bottom-anchored sheet navigation, stacked data streams, full-width single metric displays.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system prioritizes a **low-contrast outline and surface-fill** philosophy over prominent drop shadows, maintaining an official, document-centric feel.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: Muted parchment canvas (`#F7F5F0` / dark `#12181A`).
- **Level 1 (Cards, Data Panels, Form Surfaces)**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF` / dark `#1B2224`) framed by a 1px structural border (`#E0DED5` / dark `#2D3A34`). Shadows are subtle: `0 1px 2px rgba(31, 42, 36, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements, Dropdowns, Tooltips)**: Subtle tint with ambient shadow: `0 4px 12px rgba(31, 42, 36, 0.08)`, border stroke retained.
- **Level 3 (Modals, High-Priority Critical Verification Overlays)**: Crisp borders paired with an ambient backdrop scrim (`rgba(18, 24, 26, 0.6)`) and shadow: `0 12px 32px rgba(31, 42, 36, 0.16)`.
- **Status Elevation**: Rather than relying on Z-axis elevation, interactive cards and rows signal hierarchy and urgency using a **4px left border accent** in the appropriate semantic status color.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft, modern ergonomics with civic authority:

- **Cards & Data Panels**: 8px to 10px corner radius (`rounded-lg`), balancing soft edges with the structure of a legal ledger.
- **Input Fields & Action Controls**: 6px to 8px corner radius for crisp, functional interaction targets.
- **Status Chips & Verification Badges**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full`) to contrast against rectangular table rows and cards.
- **Bounding Box Highlights**: Sharp 2px radii on canvas overlays with a 2px stroke in secondary ochre (`#C17817`) and a 12% translucent fill to frame extracted text while preserving document legibility.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid brand green (`#1B5E3A`), text `#FFFFFF`, Poppins SemiBold, 8px radius, height 40px (compact 36px in data grids). Hover: 8% darker shade; Active: inset touch ring.
- **Secondary / Action**: Pure white surface, 1px border (`#E0DED5`), text `#1F2A24`. Hover: Canvas background (`#F7F5F0`).
- **Critical Reject / Flag**: Outlined warning/error actions (`#C0392B` / `#ED8936`) with 1px border stroke and 8% tinted hover state.

### 2. Status-First Cards & Table Rows
- **Pending Queue Rows**: White background with a 1px border on top, right, and bottom. The left border is a bold **4px solid accent** indicating confidence level (Green = Validated ≥85%, Amber = Flagged 60–84%, Red = Failed <60%).
- **Metric Cards**: Never use solid high-saturation fills. Use an 8% muted tint background derived from the semantic color, framed by a matching 1px border at 25% opacity. Display the metric value in Poppins SemiBold (28px) and the label in Inter Medium.

### 3. Verification Chips & Badges
- Compact pill-shaped elements (22px height).
- Format: `[Status Dot] [Confidence Score %] [Classification Label]`.
- Example: Amber dot + `72% Review` on an ochre-tinted background (`rgba(237, 137, 54, 0.1)`).

### 4. Input Fields & Verification Form Rows
- Standard inputs: 1px border (`#E0DED5`), background `#FFFFFF`, text Inter Regular. Focus state: 2px ring in primary green (`#1B5E3A`).
- **Extracted Field Pairings**: Left side shows the OCR confidence indicator chip; right side contains the editable input with an inline button to jump to the matching bounding-box coordinates on the document scan.

### 5. Multi-Step Pipeline Stepper
- A horizontal chain of 6 phases: `Upload → CV Cleanup → OCR → NLP → Validation → Scoring`.
- Completed steps display a solid green circle with a white checkmark. The current step shows an ochre-highlighted pulsing ring. Pending steps remain muted gray outlines (`#E0DED5`).

### 6. Audit Trail & Cryptographic Logs
- Chronological timeline with a continuous 2px vertical guide (`#E0DED5`).
- Each entry includes a user/role badge (e.g., `Tehsildar`, `System OCR`), a timestamp in Inter, an action summary, and a truncated 8-character SHA-256 hash rendered in **JetBrains Mono** linked via the tertiary blue (`#1B6FA8`).
